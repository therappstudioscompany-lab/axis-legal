Common questions

Restore purchases: Open the App → Unlock full access (or the paywall) → tap "Restore purchases." Use the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Cancel a subscription: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Balance. Build. Become. Cancellations and refunds for App Store purchases are handled through Apple.

Free vs paid: The free plan includes one goal and one daily action per life area. Paid plans remove free-plan limits on goals and daily actions.
