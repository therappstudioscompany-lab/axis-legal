Common questions

Restore purchases: Open Axis → Unlock Axis (or the paywall) → tap "Restore purchases." Use the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Cancel a subscription: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Axis. Cancellations and refunds for App Store purchases are handled through Apple.

Free vs paid: The free plan includes one goal and one daily action per life area. Paid plans unlock unlimited goals and daily actions.
