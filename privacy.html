Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Balance. Build. Become. (the "App") is a personal life alignment app. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

Summary

The App is designed to store your goals, daily actions, and progress locally on your device. We do not operate user accounts, and we do not sync your personal goal data to our servers.

Information stored on your device

The App may store locally:

  • Goals and daily actions you create
  • Completion history and streak-related data derived from your use
  • Onboarding and app preference settings
  • Subscription access state (free or paid)
  • A custom name for your "Other" life area, if you set one

Uninstalling the App removes this locally stored data from your device. We do not retain a separate cloud copy of your goals or progress.

Purchases

Paid plans are processed by Apple through in-app purchase. RevenueCat is used to validate subscription and lifetime access. Payment card details are handled by Apple, not by us. Apple and RevenueCat may process purchase-related identifiers (such as device or purchase tokens) needed to confirm and restore access.

See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third parties

In the current version of the App, purchase-related processing may involve:

  • Apple — App Store / in-app purchases and related account systems
  • RevenueCat — subscription and entitlement validation

We do not sell your personal information.

Analytics and tracking

The App does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the current version.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices and requests

Because goal and progress data live on your device, you can delete that data by clearing App data or uninstalling the App. For privacy questions or requests related to this policy, email us at the address below.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
